# Reel music

Drop royalty-free audio tracks here (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.aac`, `.wav`, `.ogg`).
They appear in the dashboard reel builder's **Music** picker and are mixed under
the reel with a short fade-in / fade-out.

These files are not committed (see `.gitignore`) — keep your own library local and
make sure anything you publish with is cleared for the platform.
