# Strength Training Knowledge

## Compound Movements (prioritize these)
- **Squat patterns**: Back squat, front squat, goblet squat. Primary lower body push.
- **Hinge patterns**: Deadlift, Romanian deadlift, hip thrust. Posterior chain focus.
- **Push**: Bench press, overhead press, push-ups. Upper body push.
- **Pull**: Barbell row, pull-up, lat pulldown. Upper body pull.
- **Carry/Core**: Farmer's walks, planks, pallof press. Functional stability.

## Rep/Set Schemes by Goal
- **Strength** (heavy, low rep): 3-5 sets of 3-6 reps at 80-90% 1RM. Rest 2-3 min.
- **Hypertrophy** (moderate weight, moderate rep): 3-4 sets of 8-12 reps at 65-80% 1RM. Rest 60-90s.
- **Endurance** (lighter weight, high rep): 2-3 sets of 15-20 reps at 50-65% 1RM. Rest 30-60s.
- **Power** (explosive): 3-5 sets of 3-5 reps at 60-75% 1RM, maximum speed. Rest 2-3 min.

## RPE Scale (Rate of Perceived Exertion)
- **RPE 6**: Could do 4+ more reps. Warm-up weight.
- **RPE 7**: Could do 3 more reps. Moderate effort.
- **RPE 8**: Could do 2 more reps. Hard but sustainable.
- **RPE 9**: Could do 1 more rep. Near-failure.
- **RPE 10**: Maximum effort. True failure.
- For most training: stay at RPE 7-8. Go to RPE 9-10 only during peak/testing phases.

## Common Splits
- **Full body** (2-3x/week): Best for beginners and athletes who strength train as a supplement. Hit all major patterns each session.
- **Upper/Lower** (4x/week): Upper body Mon/Thu, lower body Tue/Fri. Good balance for intermediates.
- **Push/Pull/Legs** (3-6x/week): Most volume per muscle group. Better for dedicated lifters.
- **Sport-specific** (2-3x/week): Focus on movements that transfer to the user's primary sport. E.g., runners → single-leg work, hip hinge, core.

## Sport-Specific Strength
- **Runners**: Single-leg squats, lunges, calf raises, hip thrust, core anti-rotation. 2-3 sets of 8-12 reps.
- **Cyclists**: Squats, leg press, single-leg deadlift, core planks. Higher rep ranges (10-15) to match endurance demands.
- **Swimmers**: Lat pulldown, cable row, band pull-aparts, core rotational work, shoulder stability exercises.
- **General fitness**: Full-body compound movements with progressive overload. 3 sets of 8-12 reps.

## Rest Periods
- Strength (1-5 reps): 2-5 minutes
- Hypertrophy (6-12 reps): 60-120 seconds
- Endurance (15+ reps): 30-60 seconds
- Supersets: Minimal rest between exercises, 60-90s between rounds.
